# Interview FAQ: "Why should we use your platform instead of others?"

If an interviewer or judge asks you, **"There are already GitHub stat trackers and portfolio generators out there. Why is your platform better or different?"**, here is your exact competitive advantage:

### 1. Actionable Intelligence, Not Just Vanity Metrics
Most GitHub tools (and GitHub itself) focus on **vanity metrics**—they just count stars, forks, and the number of commits. My platform evaluates **Engineering Discipline**. It looks for things like "Conventional Commits", presence of CI/CD pipelines, and proper documentation to determine if you code like a Junior or a Lead Engineer. It measures *how* you build, not just *how much* you build.

### 2. It’s an AI "CTO-in-a-Box"
Other platforms just give you a dashboard of graphs and leave it up to the recruiter to interpret the data. My platform uses Google Gemini AI to actually read the analytics and generate a **Recruiter Verdict**. It automatically acts as a virtual CTO, identifying your strongest tech stack and generating custom technical interview questions tailored specifically to your repositories.

### 3. The Project Revival Engine (Unique Feature)
Standard portfolios only show what is currently working. My platform includes a **Revival Engine** that specifically hunts down "dead" or abandoned projects and uses AI to generate step-by-step technical roadmaps to resuscitate them. It actively helps developers improve their old code to meet modern production standards—no other platform does this.

### 4. Brutally Honest "Architectural Gaps"
While other portfolio generators are designed to just make the developer look as good as possible, my analyzer is brutally honest. It actively flags **Architectural Gaps** (e.g., "Heavy reliance on JavaScript without TypeScript," or "Missing automated testing"). This is incredibly valuable for recruiters who want the truth, and for developers who want to know exactly what they need to learn to get hired.

### 5. Bridging the Gap Between "Code" and "Product"
Recruiters hate cloning repositories just to see if an app works. My platform has a dedicated **Live Project Showcase** that automatically scrapes the GitHub API for active deployment links (Vercel, Netlify, Render) and surfaces them instantly. It proves to the hiring manager that you don't just write code—you ship working products.

---
**Summary Pitch to memorize:**
*"While other tools just count your commits and stars, my platform uses AI to analyze your actual engineering discipline. It doesn't just show data; it interprets it like a Senior Technical Recruiter would, instantly bridging the gap between raw code and a hireable portfolio."*
